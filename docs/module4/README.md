# Module 4 — Explore and Configure Policies

In this module you review how policy is structured, practice safe **isolated** policy
changes, then take a guided tour of the **Default Policy** and demonstrate blocking,
redirection, and allowing a service end to end.

> **Your tenant is ephemeral and yours alone.** This writable deployment provisions a
> **dedicated tenant** just for you. You can change its policy — including the Default
> Policy — without affecting anyone else. When the UDF deployment stops, the tenant is
> **removed within about 5 minutes**. That is why the Default-Policy exercises below
> are safe here, even though you would never edit a shared or production org's Default
> Policy this way.
