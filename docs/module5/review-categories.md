# Exercise 5.1 — Review categories

**Goal:** Find where categorization signals appear in the console and understand
what category information WAIS records for an interaction.

**Prerequisites:**

- You are signed in to the admin console.
- You have at least one live event from [Module 3](/module3/README.md).

**Steps**

1. Open an activity event's details and look for **category** or **classification**
   signals (for example a content category or a service category).
2. Note how categories relate to the controls you saw in
   [Module 4](/module4/README.md) (categories are often what content controls act
   on).

> **Expected result.** You can point to where category / classification information
> is shown for an event.

**Why it matters:** Categorization is the signal that many policy actions key off
of; seeing it first makes the categorization test in
[Exercise 5.2](/module5/configure-categorization.md) meaningful.

**Reset / cleanup:** None. Read-only.

> **Note.** The exact location and naming of categorization signals, and whether a
> dedicated "categories" view exists in the writable tenant, need confirmation
> against the current UI.
